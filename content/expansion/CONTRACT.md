# Expansion pack v1 authoring contract

This pack is staged independently of the existing game's 10 recipes, 6 species, and 7 shop items. Do not edit current game files.

Recipe JSON files export arrays. Each recipe has: `id` (r- kebab-case stable ID), `name` Japanese dish name, `description` one appetizing sentence, `category` one of rice/noodles/soup/main/side/breakfast/snack, `cuisine` Japanese short name, `difficulty` 1|2|3, `rarity` common|rare|special, `minutes` positive integer including cooking/resting time, `servings` 1, `ingredients` [{name, amount}], `steps` 3-6 complete Japanese sentences actually explaining how to cook this particular dish, `tip` one specific helpful technique, `tags` 2-5 Japanese search tags, `equipment` string[], `reward` 20|40|70, `art` {vessel: bowl|plate|deep-plate|basket|board|glass, base: rice|noodles|soup|curry|salad|toast|cake|main, colors: [3-5 hex colors], garnish: string[], motif: string, toppings: string[]}. Topping and garnish names should be concise English slugs describing actual visible ingredients. `motif` identifies dish-specific visual form (e.g. triangular-onigiri, rolled-omelet, grilled-fillet, spaghetti, layered-sandwich). Names, ingredients, instructions and visual metadata must agree. No cartesian mass variants, numbered filler, or repeated generic steps. Recipes authored from scratch, not copied. Do not include nutritional/medical/allergen-free guarantees. Cook raw meat/fish/egg appropriately. Do not claim tested in a real kitchen.

Recipe group A: 150 unique rice (35), noodles (30), soup (30), breakfast (30), snack (25). IDs prefix r-. Files recipes/staples.json. Exclude the ten legacy names/IDs (egg-rice, onigiri, tofu-soup, miso-soup, fried-rice, tomato-pasta, cream-soup, curry, omurice, gratin). Can create distinct dishes in these families.
Recipe group B: 150 unique main (75), side (75). File recipes/mains-sides.json. IDs prefix r-.

Characters: 36 NEW species in characters.json, IDs c-<slug>, excluding existing komugi/mame/shizuku/yuzu/momo/goma. Fields id,name,description,personality,habitat (one of meadow/forest/seaside/market/cafe/night), favoriteCategories (2 recipe categories), favoriteTags (2-3 Japanese tags), discovery {adultCompanions:1..8, uniqueRecipes:3..80}, dialogue {greeting,fed,newDish,repeatDish}, stages [{name,description,artPath}] exactly 5, palette {body,accent,outline}, silhouette. Five stages (うまれたて, ちびっこ, わんぱく, おとな, とっておき) must change anatomy and silhouette; simple scaling or added accessories alone is insufficient. SVGs in public/expansion/assets/characters/<id>-<0|1|2|3|4>.svg. Use bold flat screenprint-style SVG, dark #202820 outlines, vermilion #df442d, leaf #24624b, cobalt #275bbb and gold #f7c544. Avoid pale pastel gradients. Invent distinct silhouettes/motifs beyond recolors. Additional visual traits/geometry fields allowed.
Items: 72 NEW cosmetic items in items.json: 36 hat, 36 room, IDs i-<slug>, no overlap legacy IDs. Fields id,name,description,kind,currency:coins|gems,price,rarity,collection (one of meadow/forest/seaside/market/cafe/night),unlock {uniqueRecipes:0..80}, artPath,renderSpec. SVGs in public/expansion/assets/items/<id>.svg. Hats must be equip-ready transparent SVG viewBox 0 0 300 300 with art in upper head area about x75..225,y0..90; room backgrounds full viewBox 0 0 800 600, clear center for pet. renderSpec {viewBox,anchor:'head'|'background',...}. Distinct meaningful designs, not bare color changes. Cosmetics don't modify XP or penalize players.
